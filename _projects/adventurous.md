Adventurous is built on a simple idea: every day should have at least one small adventure. Not a vacation,
not a road trip, just a reason to step outside your routine and find something nearby you wouldn't have
found on your own. It's out now on the App Store 🎉

## ✨ What you get

- 🗓️ **A new adventure every day**, no two days the same
- 🎯 **Personalized to your life**: it respects your work days and days off
- 📍 **Local and nearby**, scaled to how far you can realistically go
- 🌤️ **Points of interest and weather**, everything you need to make the call
- 🎢 **A fresh theme each day**: parks, museums, food, and more, without repeating recent places
- ⏰ **Daily reminders**, as early as 7 AM, plus a prompt to rate where you went
- 📖 **Your history, kept**: every adventure you took, with streaks and stats

**Platform:** iOS 26+  
**Pricing:** free 4-day trial, then $6.99/week, $59.99/year, or $119.99/year for the family
